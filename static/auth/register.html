UniPath uses your email to sign you in and your application details to prepare and track university applications. Your profile and marks are encrypted in the local demo database.

Data is used for this application journey, shown only to your signed-in account, and shared with institutions only after you explicitly choose destinations and confirm. No data is sent to a real university or NSFAS in this prototype. You can access, correct, export, or delete your demo information from your profile.
