# Programme catalogue notes

## Source and organization

The programme catalogue is organized across the nine faculties listed in the University of Pretoria's [2026 Yearbooks](https://www.up.ac.za/yearbooks/home):

1. Economic and Management Sciences
2. Education
3. Engineering, Built Environment and Information Technology
4. Health Sciences
5. Humanities
6. Law
7. Natural and Agricultural Sciences
8. Theology and Religion
9. Veterinary Science

Programme names are drawn from the Yearbook undergraduate programme lists. The Yearbook is the source of programme naming and grouping, not of the prototype's displayed admission thresholds. UP notes that programme names can change; prospective students must check current requirements and rules in official university sources.

## What the demo shows

Each programme entry has a faculty, plain-language description, example careers, and illustrative match data for UP and a fictional second institution. The careers are orientation examples, not career guarantees. Regulated careers may require further study, registration, or professional examinations.

The demo uses a seven-subject matric record including Life Orientation and captures APS separately. This reflects the agreed student profile requirement. Institutions calculate APS according to their own rules; Life Orientation is excluded from APS calculations for some UP programmes, including BNurs. The prototype does not calculate or validate official APS values.

## Accuracy boundary

- Catalogue entries should be checked against the current Yearbook before students rely on them.
- APS, English, and Mathematics thresholds in `app.py` are hand-authored illustrative demo values, not UP admissions criteria.
- Match results are a demonstration of interface behavior, not admissions advice or a prediction of acceptance.
- Central Demo University is fictional and its thresholds are also illustrative.
- The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Pretoria.

## Refresh workflow

When refreshing the demo catalogue, compare programme name, faculty, undergraduate availability, and current Yearbook cycle. Keep the year in source notes and update this document and the UI notice. Do not replace illustrative thresholds with official criteria without verified institution-provided data and permission to use it.
